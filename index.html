About VERITAS AI

VERITAS AI is a next-generation forensic detection user interface designed to identify synthetic media generated by diffusion models (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, DALL-E), neural face re-enactment tools, and voice cloning models.

Detection Methodology

  • Image Forensics: Analyzes spatial pixel coherence, frequency domain (FFT) noise patterns, and PRNU sensor fingerprints.
  • Video Forensics: Tracks facial landmark trajectory stability, optical flow continuity, and lip-sync phoneme synchronization.
  • Audio Forensics: Detects vocoder phase harmonics, absence of natural glottal airflow variation, and spectral roll-off.

Backend Integration Ready

Currently running in client-side simulation mode for demonstrative and portfolio purposes. To attach a real Python/PyTorch or Flask backend, simply update the endpoint URL in js/api.js.
